Where a puzzle comes from

How Lichess turns chess games into puzzles, next to how tinue does it for Tak. The main split: Lichess trusts an engine's evaluation and demands a single right move at every step. Tinue proves the forced road and accepts every winning move. It checks after the fact whether the position actually swung the game.

Lichess (chess)
tinue (Tak)
Games
different scale
Lichess

Monthly dumps of rated games, but only games Lichess has already analysed with Stockfish (the move text carries evals).

About 600 million analysed games scanned. Games are graded by both players' ratings and the time control. The lowest grade (a player at 1500 or below, or a time control lasting about a minute or less) is skipped. Lower grades only yield longer puzzles.
tinue

The PlayTak dump: 880,521 games. Rated, swap opening, standard piece counts, no guests, sizes 4×4 to 8×8.

The live miner has no rating floor and uses road wins only. The deep and swing miners require both players at 1500+ and stick to 5×5 and 6×6.
Finding the moment
opposite direction
Lichess

Scan forward. Candidate: the opponent's move makes the solver's winning chance jump by more than 0.6 (on a −1 to 1 scale), or reveals a mate in 14 or fewer.

Rejected if the solver was already above +3 pawns before the mistake (unless it's now a short mate), or already up material.
tinue
Live: walk back from the final road 1, 3 and 5 plies. Take the earliest position with a forced road.
Deep: keep walking back, 7–15 plies, until the road is no longer forced. mined, not live
Swing: scan forward like Lichess with Tiltak: the solver jumps by at least 0.30, or the loser drops by that much. Then prove the road. mining now
What “the solution works” means
eval vs proof
Lichess

Stockfish, up to 25M nodes per move, best two lines. Mate puzzles must reach checkmate. Advantage puzzles must stay at +2 pawns or better and end when the next move is no longer the only one.

Endgames with 7 or fewer pieces check the tablebase first.
tinue

A proof, not an estimate: every line ends in a road (or a flat win). Exhaustive search up to 5 plies. Proof-number search (df-pn) for 7–15 plies, with an exact shortest length.

No “advantage” puzzles. A road is reached or it isn't.
Other good moves
opposite policy
Lichess

Every solver move must be the only good move: winning chance at least 0.7 above the second-best. A mate line with another mate at any non-final step is thrown out.

The exception: on the last move, any checkmate counts.
tinue

Every move that wins in the shortest time is stored and accepted. A slower win gets “That wins too, but slower” with no penalty to the solver.

The puzzle itself is served less often (+100 handicap). Unique first move: 61% of the live set, 71% of the deep mine.
Opponent's replies
different aim
Lichess

Whatever Stockfish plays.

tinue

The most stubborn reply: survive longest, then leave the fewest winning answers, then follow the real game. Precomputed, so the browser never searches during the puzzle.

Did it change the game?
same question, asked later
Lichess

Built into detection: the eval jump is the turning point.

tinue

A separate scoring pass with Tiltak over the proved puzzles, refined three times. A turning point needs all of these:

the solver was at most 50% before the mistake; the loser wasn't already lost (a proof); the loser had an escape (where that can be checked exactly); no non-winning move scores 0.65 or higher; no slower wins. Puzzles that fail are served less, not deleted. Live: 1,836 turning points.
Themes
same approach
Lichess

A rule-based tagger over the solution: about 30 motifs (fork, pin, skewer, sacrifice, zugzwang…) plus mate patterns. Players can vote themes up or down.

tinue

The same idea with Tak motifs: fork, smash, throw, spread, reveal, wall block, stack road, gaelet, quiet move, dragon. No theme voting.

Starting rating
prior vs flat
Lichess

Every puzzle starts at 1500 ± 500 and settles from players' attempts (Glicko-2).

tinue

A guess from the puzzle's features, ± 250: 900 / 1500 / 2100 for 1 / 3 / 5 plies, deep 2400–2850. Nudges for smash, throws, quiet moves, board size, search effort and how many first moves win. Then Glicko-2 like Lichess.

Too few players to start everything at 1500 and let them sort it out.
Quality control and serving
votes vs handicaps
Lichess

No human review today. Up/down votes are weighted by player trust, and the bottom 5% by vote is never served. A hand-kept blocklist removes bad puzzles. Puzzles are served from rating buckets.

tinue

No votes. The closest rating to yours wins after handicaps: turning points first, unscored +150, flawed +100, “just converting a won game” +250.

Why tinue proves instead of estimating

A Tak road is a yes/no fact a search can certify within 15 plies. A proof makes every puzzle sound whatever an engine's evaluation says. Tiltak is then used only to judge whether the moment mattered.

Why tinue accepts every win

Tak positions often have several roads. Lichess's only-move rule would discard about 40% of our multi-move puzzles, which are perfectly good to solve.

What tinue borrowed

The forward eval-jump scan (swing miner), the rule-based tagger, Glicko-2 with first-attempt-only rating, and damped puzzle ratings when a theme hints at the answer.
